# Tone Categories

Map the user's "main impression" (and optional "accent nuance") to one category each. The main category sets the hero image style and the copy voice; the accent only adds small touches (image details, word choice). Never let the accent override the main category.

| Category | Trigger words | Hero image direction | Copy voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light & modern | casual, friendly, stylish, fresh, cafe | soft natural light, muted blues/greens, airy space | relaxed, conversational |
| Smart & clean | sleek, tidy, intellectual, B2B, minimal | white/grey, one accent color, generous negative space | precise, short sentences |
| Pop & friendly | energetic, bright, fun, colorful | saturated primaries, playful composition | upbeat, exclamation-free but lively |
| Cute & sweet | cute, pastel, feminine, soft | pastel palette, rounded soft forms | warm, gentle |
| Corporate & trust | trustworthy, reliable, formal, safe | navy/deep blue, orderly composition | steady, factual |
| Premium & luxury | high-end, refined, upscale | black/dark grey with gold accents, dramatic light | restrained, understated |
| Tech & cyber | futuristic, AI, digital, innovative | dark mode, vivid blue/neon glow | crisp, forward-looking |
| Natural & organic | natural, earthy, warm, organic | beige/khaki/earth tones, natural textures | calm, unhurried |
| Active & powerful | sporty, bold, high-impact | red/black, diagonal motion, high contrast | direct, punchy |
| Retro & vintage | retro, nostalgic, classic | muted retro palette, film grain | playful, nostalgic |
| Promo & sale | deal, sale, limited-time, campaign | high-contrast yellow/red, bold focal subject | urgent but honest, price/benefit first |

If the impression doesn't match any row, pick the closest and say which you chose.

## Avoid list (the "never" tone)

Ask for one tone to avoid. Translate it into a ban plus a replacement direction:

| Avoid | Ban | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Flashy / cluttered | busy backgrounds, more than one focal subject | one subject, calm background |
| Cheap | harsh saturation, stock-clip-art feel | restrained palette, clean light |
| Stiff / too serious | cold, corporate phrasing | friendlier verbs, human scenes |
| Childish / too pop | cartoonish shapes, neon palettes | refined palette, grounded imagery |
| Other (free text) | whatever the user wrote | opposite of it |

Put the ban in the image prompt ("no ...") and respect it in the copy.
